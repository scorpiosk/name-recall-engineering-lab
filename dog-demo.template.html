Training evidence, limitations & how to reproduce
Recorded training loss · answer + stop token

This is a genuine decoder-only neural network with learned embeddings, positional embeddings, five pre-normalized attention/MLP blocks, residual connections, a final normalization, and a vocabulary output head. It was trained with cross-entropy loss on the name and end-of-sequence tokens. It is a narrow educational model, not a general-purpose or frontier-scale LLM.

Rows 1–4 apply the same final normalization and output head to intermediate vectors. Those readouts help inspect the computation; only the output after layer 5 is the trained final prediction. Confidence need not rise with every layer. Inputs outside the example patterns may fail.
